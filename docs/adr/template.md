# The decision, as a statement in the present tense

One or two paragraphs: what the situation was, what was decided, and what that buys. Write the decision, not
the discussion that led to it. A reader who knows nothing about the change should finish this section knowing
what the repository now does and why that is not an accident.

## Considered options

Only the alternatives worth remembering, one bold sentence each plus why it lost. Delete this section when
there was no real alternative.

**The obvious other way.** Rejected: the concrete cost, measured rather than assumed.

## Consequences

Only what is not obvious from the decision itself: what becomes harder, what somebody will trip over, what has
to change together from now on. Delete this section when the decision speaks for itself.

- The thing that now has to be done in two places at once.
- The check that fails when somebody forgets.
